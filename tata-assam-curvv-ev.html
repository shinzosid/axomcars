Tata Curvv.ev FAQs

The Tata Curvv.ev 55 variant offers a certified MIDC range of 502 km. In real-world conditions (C75), you can expect a range of approximately 400 - 425 km, making it one of the longest-range EVs in its class.

Yes, the Curvv.ev features bi-directional charging technology. It supports Vehicle-to-Load (V2L) at 3.3kVA and Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) charging at 5kVA, allowing you to power appliances or charge another EV.

The Tata Curvv.ev comes with two battery pack options: a 45 kWh pack for the Medium Range variants and a 55 kWh pack for the Long Range variants, offering up to 502 km of certified range.

Yes, the top-end Accomplished variants of the Tata Curvv come with a gesture-controlled powered tailgate, allowing you to open the boot hands-free, which is a segment-first feature.

The Tata Curvv.ev offers a certified range of up to 585 km for the larger battery variant. In real-world highway and city conditions across Assam, you can comfortably expect around 400-420 km on a single charge.

Yes! The Curvv.ev supports advanced V2L and V2V charging. You can power standard electrical appliances (laptops, camping lights, speakers) directly from the car's charging port during weekend getaways in the hills.

The Curvv.ev features a high 190mm ground clearance and an IP67-rated waterproof and dustproof enclosure for its high-voltage battery pack and motor. This ensures safe, trouble-free operation on flooded Guwahati roads during heavy rains.
