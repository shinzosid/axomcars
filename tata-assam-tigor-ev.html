Tata Tigor.ev FAQs

The Tata Tigor.ev comes with a high-energy density 26 kWh battery pack that offers an ARAI-certified range of 315 km on a full charge, making it ideal for both city commutes and short highway trips around Assam.

The Tigor.ev supports DC fast charging, allowing you to charge from 10% to 80% in just 59 minutes. A standard 15A plug point at home will fully charge the car in about 9.4 hours.

Ziptron is Tata's advanced EV powertrain that features a high-voltage system, permanent magnet synchronous motor, and IP67 rated battery pack, ensuring zippy performance and long-term durability.
