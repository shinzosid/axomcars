Documents Required for Car Loan

Please keep these documents ready to fast-track your loan approval process.

Company / Business / Self Employed

  • ✓ Company PAN Card
  • ✓ GST Registration
  • ✓ Trade License
  • ✓ ITR for Last 2/3 years with Balance Sheet, Computation with Profit & Loss (Audited ITR)
  • ✓ Current Bank Account Statement for Last 6 Months
  • ✓ MOU (If Partnership Company)
  • ✓ Guarantor PAN & Aadhar (Partner)
  • ✓ Form 26 AS Last 2/3 Years (TDS)

Government Employee

  • ✓ PAN Card
  • ✓ Aadhar Card
  • ✓ Salary Slip 3 to 6 months (Varies with Bank)
  • ✓ Bank Statement for Last 6 to 12 Months (Varies with Bank)
  • ✓ ID Card
  • ✓ Form 16 (Varies with Bank)

Private Employee

  • ✓ PAN Card
  • ✓ Aadhar Card
  • ✓ Salary Slip 3 to 6 months (Varies with Bank)
  • ✓ Bank Statement for Last 6 to 12 Months (Varies with Bank)
  • ✓ ID Card
  • ✓ ITR LAST 2 Years if Available
