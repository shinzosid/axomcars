Tata Tiago.ev FAQs

The Tata Tiago.ev is built on a high-strength steel structure and has been awarded a 4-star safety rating from Global NCAP. It comes with dual airbags, ABS with EBD, and Ziptron technology that is IP67 rated for water and dust resistance.

The Tata Tiago.ev offers two range options: the Long Range (LR) variant provides an MIDC certified range of 293 km, while the Medium Range (MR) variant offers 223 km. Real-world range is approximately 205 km and 168 km respectively.

The Tiago.ev can be charged from 10% to 80% in just 58 minutes using a DC fast charger. Using a standard 15A home socket, it takes about 6.9 hours (MR) to 8.7 hours (LR) for a full charge.

Using a standard 15A home socket, the Tiago.ev Long Range charges from 10% to 100% in about 8.7 hours. A 7.2 kW AC fast wallbox charger reduces this time to around 3.6 hours.
