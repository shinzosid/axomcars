Tata Tiago FAQs

The Tata Tiago has been awarded a 4-star safety rating from Global NCAP, making it one of the safest hatchbacks in its segment. It features a strong body structure, dual airbags, and ABS with EBD as standard.

The Tata Tiago.ev offers two range options: the Long Range (LR) variant provides an MIDC certified range of 293 km, while the Medium Range (MR) variant offers 223 km. Real-world range is approximately 205 km and 168 km respectively.

The top-end variants of the Tata Tiago feature an industry-leading 8-speaker surround sound system by Harman, providing a premium audio experience for all passengers.
