Tata Safari vs Mahindra XUV700: The Battle of 3-Row Flagship SUVs in Assam

For joint families in Assam, buying a flagship 3-row SUV is a major milestone. The Tata Safari and Mahindra XUV700 represent the peak of Indian automotive engineering, offering premium comfort, high safety, and advanced technology. Whether you are planning family holidays to Kaziranga, traveling across Northeast state capitals, or driving daily in Guwahati, you need an SUV that handles all three rows with comfort and safety. This dealer guide from Axom Cars compares their seating space, safety, and features.

Quick Verdict

Genuinely helping buyers decide which SUV matches their style and budget.

TATA MOTORS ADVANTAGE

Choose Tata Safari if:

  • You want a spacious third row that is comfortable for adults on long trips.
  • You prefer the luxury of individual ventilated captain seats in the second row.
  • You want a robust Land Rover-derived platform and a 5-star B-NCAP safety rating.
  • You need terrain modes (Wet/Rough) to handle monsoon driving in Assam.
COMPETITOR ADVANTAGE

Choose Mahindra XUV700 if:

  • You want a highly refined and powerful petrol engine option.
  • You prefer a silent cabin with minimal diesel engine sound.
  • You want high-tech features like smart flush door handles.
  • You prefer a softer suspension setup for city driving.

The Bottom Line

Choose the Tata Safari for adult-friendly third-row space, second-row captain seat luxury, robust build, and terrain modes. Choose the Mahindra XUV700 if you want petrol refinement, a silent cabin, and soft city ride quality.

LOCAL ROAD ANALYSIS

Which is Better for Assam Roads & Weather?

Assam's state highways and single-lane roads can be challenging, especially when carrying a full load of passengers. The Tata Safari's Land Rover D8-derived OMEGARC platform gives it high stability. It remains planted and composed at high speeds, even with all seven seats occupied. Its 205mm ground clearance is helpful when crossing flooded road sections in Guwahati or navigating rough margins. The Mahindra XUV700 is comfortable in the city, but its softer suspension can cause rear-seat passengers to feel body roll around sharp highway bends. The Safari's Wet and Rough terrain modes adjust ESP braking slip to provide extra traction, which is helpful on muddy roads during the monsoon.

OWNERSHIP COSTS

Running Cost & Maintenance Comparison

The Safari is powered by a Fiat-sourced 2.0L Kryotec diesel engine that averages around 11-13 km/l in Guwahati and 15-17 km/l on open highways. The XUV700 offers both petrol and diesel engines. Its mStallion petrol engine is highly refined but has high fuel consumption in the city (8-10 km/l). Its diesel engine has similar running costs to the Safari. CABIN COMFORT

Interior Space, Legroom & Family Comfort

Interior comfort is where the Safari behaves like a flagship lounge. It offers a 6-seater version with individual, ventilated captain seats in the second row, which is a segment-first feature. It also has a spacious third row with dedicated AC vents, blower controls, and USB ports, making it suitable for adults on long trips. The XUV700 only offers a second-row bench seat, and its third row is cramped, making it suitable mostly for children on short trips.

SAFETY RATINGS

Safety Ratings, Build Quality & Airbags

Both SUVs have earned 5-star crash safety ratings. The Safari was tested under the newer, stricter Bharat NCAP norms and scored top marks. It features 7 airbags, Level 2 ADAS (including Autonomous Emergency Braking, Blind Spot Detection, and Rear Cross Traffic Alert), and a strong passenger cage. The XUV700 also offers Level 2 ADAS and up to 7 airbags. The Safari's Land Rover-derived platform gives it a slight edge in structural strength.

CABIN TECH

Technology, Infotainment & Connected Tech

The Safari features a 12.3-inch touchscreen infotainment system, a 10.25-inch digital instrument cluster, a 10-speaker JBL audio system, and connected car tech. The XUV700 has dual 10.25-inch screens and an Alexa-enabled voice assistant. The Safari's infotainment system is larger and has a sharper UI, while the XUV700's Alexa integration is highly convenient.
