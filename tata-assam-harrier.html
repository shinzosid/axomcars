Tata Harrier FAQs

The Tata Harrier, powered by the Kryotec 2.0L Diesel engine, delivers a competitive mileage of approximately 14-16 km/l, depending on driving conditions and transmission type (Manual or Automatic).

Yes, the new Tata Harrier comes equipped with Level 2 ADAS in its Fearless variants, offering features like Autonomous Emergency Braking, Forward Collision Warning, and Traffic Sign Recognition for enhanced safety.

The Harrier is available in several striking colors, including Sunbeam Yellow, Coral Red, Pebble Grey, and the exclusive Oberon Black in the #DARK edition. Visit our Guwahati showrooms to see them in person.
