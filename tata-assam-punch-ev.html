Tata Punch.ev FAQs

The acti.ev is Tata's first pure EV architecture. It enables a flat floor, maximized cabin space, and 15% better efficiency compared to converted platforms, while supporting advanced features like V2L and ADAS.

The Punch.ev 40 variant comes with an industry-leading Lifetime Warranty on the HV battery for the first owner. Other variants offer a robust warranty of 8 years or 1,60,000 km, whichever is earlier.

Yes, the Punch.ev supports DC fast charging, which can charge the battery from 10% to 80% in approximately 56 minutes. It also comes with a 7.2 kW AC fast charger option for quicker home charging.

Using the standard 3.3 kW home charger, the Punch.ev Long Range charges from 10% to 100% in about 9.4 hours. If you install the optional 7.2 kW fast AC wallbox, it takes approximately 5 hours.

Yes. The battery and traction motor are IP67 certified, meaning they are completely dustproof and waterproof. It can safely navigate waterlogged city segments during heavy monsoons.

The Punch.ev Long Range offers a certified range of 421 km, delivering a real-world range of approximately 290-310 km in city traffic on a single charge.
