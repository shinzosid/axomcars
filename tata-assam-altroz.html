Assam's national highways demand robust vehicle safety. The Altroz is built on the high-strength ALFA architecture, earning a full 5-star Global NCAP safety rating to keep your family protected on long highway runs.

Navigating the bumper-to-bumper traffic on GS Road and Zoo Road is effortless with the advanced wet-clutch DCA (Dual Clutch Automatic) transmission. It shifts gears seamlessly without overheating in city crawl speeds.
