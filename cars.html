Vehicle Range FAQs

Which Tata SUV is best for families in Assam?

The Tata Safari and Harrier are excellent choices for families, offering spacious interiors and top-notch safety features. For smaller families, the Tata Nexon and Punch provide great versatility and performance on Assam's diverse terrains.

Are Tata Electric Vehicles (EVs) available at Axom Cars?

Yes, we offer the full range of Tata EVs, including the Nexon.ev, Punch.ev, and Tiago.ev. Visit our showrooms in Dispur or Ulubari to experience the future of sustainable driving.

What is the starting price of Tata cars at Axom Cars?

Prices vary by model and variant. The Tata Tiago starts at an attractive price point for budget-conscious buyers, while premium models like the Safari cater to luxury seekers. Contact us for the latest ex-showroom and on-road prices in Guwahati.
