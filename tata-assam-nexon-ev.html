Tata Nexon.ev FAQs

With a DC fast charger, the Nexon.ev 45 can charge from 10% to 80% in just 40 minutes. For home charging, the 7.2kW AC wall box charger can fully charge the vehicle in approximately 6 hours.

The new Nexon.ev 45 variant offers a class-leading MIDC certified range of 489 km. In real-world C75 conditions, you can expect approximately 350-370 km on a single charge.

The new Tata Nexon has been awarded a prestigious 5-star safety rating from Global NCAP and B-NCAP, making it one of the safest compact SUVs in India.
