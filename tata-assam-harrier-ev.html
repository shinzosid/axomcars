Tata Harrier.ev FAQs

The Tata Harrier.ev offers a segment-best MIDC certified range of up to 627 km. In real-world C75 driving conditions, you can expect a range of approximately 450-500 km on a full charge.

Yes, the new Tata Harrier comes equipped with Level 2 ADAS in its Fearless variants, offering features like Autonomous Emergency Braking, Forward Collision Warning, and Traffic Sign Recognition for enhanced safety.

Yes, the Harrier.ev features an advanced Dual Motor AWD (QWD) system on the acti.ev platform, providing exceptional grip and performance across varied terrains in Assam.

Using a high-speed DC fast charger along the national highway network in Assam, the Harrier.ev can charge from 10% to 80% in approximately 45-50 minutes. A standard 7.2 kW home charger will fully charge the battery overnight.

Guwahati monsoons and hill roads can become extremely slippery. The dual-motor AWD system provides active torque distribution to the wheels with the most grip, preventing slides and wheel spin on muddy or water-soaked tracks.
