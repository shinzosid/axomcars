Technical Specifications

Built on the OMEGARC architecture derived from Land Rover's D8 Platform.

Engine & Transmission

  • Engine Type: Kryotec 2.0L BS6 Ph 2 Turbocharged
  • Max Power: 125 kW (170PS) @ 3750 rpm
  • Max Torque: 350 Nm @ 1750 - 2500 rpm
  • Displacement: 1956 CC, In-line 4 Cylinder
  • Transmission: 6 Speed Manual / 6 Speed Automatic

Dimensions & Capacity

  • Length x Width x Height: 4605 x 1922 x 1718 mm
  • Wheelbase: 2741 mm
  • Boot Space: 445 Litres (815L Folded)

  • Seating Capacity: 5 Seater

Suspension & Brakes
